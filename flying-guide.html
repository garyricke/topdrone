Section 01 — Pre-Flight Ritual

Before the Motors Spin.

Complete these steps in order, every flight. Skip one and you risk a gimbal strain error, a botched Return-to-Home, or a lost aircraft. Discipline on the ground buys forgiveness in the air.

01
Remove the Gimbal Protector
Forgetting this strains the single-axis camera gimbal and can quickly trigger an ESC or motor overheat error. Pop it off before anything else.
02
Inspect the Props
Check the built-in cinewhoop prop guards for debris. Run a finger along the edge of each propeller to feel for hairline cracks or chips.
03
Power Sequence
Goggles 3 first (press once to check battery, then hold 2 seconds), then the RC Motion 3, then the Avata 2 drone. Reverse order on shutdown.
04
Acquire Satellites
Wait on the ground until your goggles show 10–12 satellites and the Home Point is updated. Take off too early and RTH will not work correctly.
Critical — Home Point If the Home Point is not set before takeoff, your drone has nowhere to fly back to. In a signal-loss event, it will hover and drain its battery instead of returning to you.
Section 02 — Mastering Goggles 3

Dial in the Picture.

The Goggles 3 feature dual 1920×1080 Micro-OLED displays at up to 100 Hz. FPV flight rises and falls on this visual link — a blurry or off-axis image is a crash waiting to happen.

Physical Calibration
A
Diopter Adjustment
Range: -6.0 D to +2.0 D. Open Settings > Display > Focus Adjustment, close your left eye, turn the right knob until the text is razor-sharp. Repeat for the other eye.
B
IPD Adjustment
Slide the knobs horizontally (56 mm to 72 mm) until display corners aren't cut off and your eyes don't strain to merge the image. Get this right or you'll fly with a headache.
C
Real View PiP
Double-tap firmly on the right side of the goggles' plastic frame (or double-press the dial on Motion 3) to overlay the real world via the front-facing stereo cameras.
D
3D Real View — Advanced
Go to Settings > Display and switch Real View from 2D to 3D. Both external cameras run simultaneously, preserving depth when you reach for a water bottle.
Camera & OSD Tweak Guide

Default settings are tuned for casual users. These are the changes that separate cinematic footage from forgettable footage.

Menu Path Setting Beginner Setup Advanced Setup Why It Matters
Display Sharpness 4 or 5 Reduce to 2 or 3 Default sharpness creates jagged digital edges. Lowering it yields a smoother, more cinematic look.
Camera > DVR Recording Bitrate 30 Mbps 60 Mbps High motion or heavy foliage will pixelate and produce compression artifacts at low bitrates.
Camera > DVR Record OSD Disabled Enabled Overlaying telemetry onto your SD card footage creates a diagnostic trail if you crash.
Camera Color Profile Standard D-Log M (10-bit) Standard bakes in a finished color curve. D-Log M captures flat footage with high dynamic range for grading in post.
Camera EIS RockSteady EIS Off (Gyroflow) RockSteady stabilizes instantly. EIS off preserves raw gyro data so you can stabilize footage perfectly in Gyroflow.
O4 Signal Optimization

By default, the O4 transmission is set to Auto, hopping between 2.4 GHz and 5.8 GHz. Here's when to override it.

Mode 01
Flying Solo
Keep transmission on Auto. Open up the bandwidth to unlock the crispest, lowest-latency feed possible. No one else's signal to step on.
Bandwidth: 50–60 MHz
Mode 02
Flying With Others
Switch to Manual. Drop bandwidth low and lock onto an assigned channel. This prevents your transmission from overpowering and blinding other pilots' video feeds.
Bandwidth: 20 MHz, locked channel
Section 03 — Piloting with RC Motion 3

Gestures Become Flight.

The RC Motion 3 turns hand gestures into flight commands via an internal IMU. It uses a target-matching system — your wrist points, the drone follows. Master the trigger and you've mastered the controller.

Hardware Layout
5D Joystick
Vertical: Up/Down altitude. Horizontal: Strafe left/right. Pushes are linear and maintain the drone's heading.
Lock Button
Double press: Arm motors. Hold: Auto Takeoff / Landing. Single press: Emergency brake (instant hover).
Mode Button
Tap: Toggle Normal / Sport mode. Hold: Initiate Return-to-Home.
Thumb Dial
Scroll: Camera gimbal tilt up/down. Double press: Open Real View PiP.
Shutter / Record
Tap: Take a photo or start/stop video. Hold: Toggle between photo and video modes.
Accelerator Trigger
Dual-stop tactile pull. First stop holds position; second stop engages propulsion. Push forward (away from palm) to fly backward.
The Dual-Stop Trigger
First stop — locked hover. Second stop — full propulsion.
First Stop — Light Squeeze Locks the drone's position in space while letting you twist your wrist to pan or tilt the camera gimbal. The drone stays stationary. Perfect for framing a shot.
Second Stop — Full Squeeze Engages the propulsion. The drone flies directly toward the white target circle projected in your goggles — this is the actual go-pedal.
Reverse Flight Push the trigger forward (away from your palm) to fly backward. Subtle but critical when you need to back out of a tight reveal shot.
Easy ACRO

Flick the 5D joystick down during flight to open quick settings and select Easy ACRO. This profile lets beginners mimic freestyle pilots through automated acrobatics.

Joystick Left or Right
Slide Roll
Flick left or right to execute a clean 360° axial roll. The signature freestyle move — instant cinematic punch.
Joystick Up or Down
Front / Back Flip
Flick up or down for a perfect vertical loop. Make sure you have altitude — the drone will dip several feet before recovering.
Velocity + Flick
180° Drift
Push forward velocity high, then flick left or right. The drone sweeps through a 180° power slide while preserving momentum.
Section 04 — Manual FPV Tuning

Twin Sticks. Total Control.

If you're transitioning away from Motion 3 to absolute acrobatic control, you'll need the traditional twin-stick DJI FPV Remote Controller 3. There's a hardware modification to perform before the software switch.

Peel back the left rear rubber grip to reveal the F1 / F2 friction screws.
Mechanical Mod — Friction Throttle

True manual flight requires a non-centering throttle. Peel back the left rear rubber grip on the Remote Controller 3 and locate the screws labeled F1 and F2.

Screw F1
Add Friction
Tighten F1 to increase resistance on the left throttle stick. Set it heavy enough that the stick doesn't drift, but light enough to move smoothly.
Screw F2
Kill the Spring
Tighten F2 until the self-centering spring is fully disabled. The left stick should now slide smoothly and remain exactly where you leave it.
Enable Manual Mode

Connect your controller, power up, then go to Settings > Control > Remote Controller > Flight Mode and switch to Manual Mode.

Warning for Beginners Do not attempt manual mode in the real world without practicing first. Download an FPV simulator (Liftoff, Uncrashed, or The Drone Racing League Sim), plug your Remote Controller 3 into your computer via USB-C, and practice hovering and leveling out for at least 5 to 10 hours before you ever leave the ground.
Section 05 — FPV Emergency Protocols

When It All Goes Wrong.

When you're flying FPV, muscle memory during emergencies will save your aircraft. Three protocols. Learn them cold — you won't have time to think when you need them.

The orange Lock / Pause button — your panic anchor.
Protocol 01
The Emergency Brake
Tap the orange Lock / Pause button on either controller. No matter your speed, angle, or orientation, the drone stops instantly, levels out, and reverts to a rock-solid Normal-mode hover.
Protocol 02
Signal Loss Action
Configured at Settings > Safety > Advanced Safety Settings. Choose Return-to-Home or Hover — and choose deliberately. The wrong choice in the wrong environment is a guaranteed crash.
Protocol 03
Turtle Mode
Clipped an obstacle and landed upside down? Don't walk out to flip it. Open the goggles shortcut menu, select Turtle Mode, and two motors reverse to flip the Avata 2 right-side up remotely.
Signal Loss — RTH or Hover?
Best For Open Skies
Return To Home
Outdoor cinematic flying. If the drone loses link, it climbs to a configured altitude and flies straight back to the Home Point. Make sure that altitude clears every tree and pole on the route.
Mandatory For Indoors
Hover In Place
Indoors, inside abandoned buildings, or under dense canopy. If the drone tries to ascend to its RTH altitude here, it will smash into the ceiling, rafters, or branches.
Pro Habit Map Turtle Mode to a dedicated button in Settings > Control. When you're upside down in the weeds, you don't want to be hunting through a menu — you want one button press to recover.
